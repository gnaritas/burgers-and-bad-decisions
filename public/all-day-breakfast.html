Waffles at 11 p.m.? On the menu. Burgers & Bad Decisions is planning to open around 5 a.m. and close around midnight — built for the people who eat before sunrise and the ones who eat after midnight.

“I used to work at the Penobscot County Jail, and I remember how hard it was to finish a long shift and find a convenient place to eat.”

Shianne Levesque, owner — via the Bangor Daily News (paraphrased in coverage)

Why the hours are backwards

Downtown Bangor doesn’t have much open at 5 a.m. or at 11:30 p.m. The plan is to be the place that is — first responders and early shifts in the morning, families before school, and the post-bar crowd late, with the storefront sitting right next to downtown’s bar block.

Opening hours aren’t final yet — the posted plan is roughly 5 a.m. to midnight, with the shop staying open a bit later on weekend nights. The Facebook page carries the confirmed schedule at opening.

Hungry at an ungodly hour?

That’s the entire business plan. Opening soon at 68 Main Street.
