What kind of place is this?

A family-friendly burger bar on Main Street — in the old Taste of India space — built for the people who work early, work late, and everyone who just wants dinner without cooking it.

  • Early & late. The plan is to open around 5 a.m. and close around midnight — breakfast for the 5 a.m. shift, burgers for the post-bar crowd.
  • Loaded, not fussy. Signature half-pound burgers, tenders, wings with seven sauces, appetizers.
  • Somebody for everyone. A vegetarian version of everything, and a non-alcoholic version of every signature drink.

“The bad decisions [name] is because it’s not a completely healthy menu, and we are a bar as well. A lot of our signature drinks are sweet and sugary, and a lot of our signature burgers are very loaded with stuff.”

Shianne Levesque, owner — via the Bangor Daily News

First responders eat like locals

Shianne worked at the Penobscot County Jail — she remembers finishing a long shift with nowhere decent to eat. A discount for first responders, law enforcement and veterans is planned for the opening.

Mondays, the little ones eat free

Every item on the kids’ menu is planned free on Mondays. “It hits personally, because I am a single mother,” Shianne says.

Bring quarters for the arcade

About half a dozen arcade games are planned for the front of the restaurant — dinner and a game, Main Street style.

“I want you to feel at home.”

Opening soon at 68 Main Street. Hours, the final menu, and the opening-night call all go up on the shop’s Facebook page.
