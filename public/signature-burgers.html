THE HEADLINES

Three mistakes
worth making

Half-pound patties, loaded on purpose. “A lot of our signature burgers are very loaded with stuff,” says owner Shianne Levesque. Every one has a vegetarian version.

No. 001

The Classic Mistake V

A half-pound cheeseburger. No gimmicks — just the biggest, juiciest thing in the case, done right. This is the one you order after you’ve tried everything else.

1⁄2 lb patty · cheese · burger

No. 002

The Bad Decision V

The namesake energy: a half-pound bacon cheddar burger with an onion ring and BBQ sauce. Sweet, smoky, absurd. Exactly the decision you make at 10 p.m. and don’t regret.

1⁄2 lb patty · bacon · cheddar · onion ring · BBQ

No. 003

The Hangover Burger V

A half-pound cheeseburger with an egg and bacon. Medical science, Main Street edition. Breakfast and dinner collide on a plate — which makes sense in a place that serves both around the clock.

1⁄2 lb patty · egg · bacon · cheese
