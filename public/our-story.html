A very good
bad decision

Burgers & Bad Decisions is one locally owned restaurant — no chain, no franchise — at 68 Main Street in downtown Bangor, in the space the Taste of India family ran until their retirement in late September 2026.

Where it came from

Shianne Levesque spent years working at the Penobscot County Jail — long shifts that ended at hours when nothing downtown was open. She remembered exactly what that felt like, and “it’s been something I’ve wanted to do for a while.”

She chose 68 Main Street because the space is on the smaller side: comfortable and homey, not a warehouse. New kitchen equipment went in, fresh paint went up, and the rest came together around one idea — a place that feeds the early shift and the late one, and treats both like regulars.

Who it’s for

“This is a safe place for families and friends to hang out and just anybody, to be honest, whether you’re 2 years old or you’re 90 years old,” Shianne says. “I want you to feel at home.”

She’s a single mother — which is why every kids’ menu item is planned free on Mondays. A discount for first responders, law enforcement and veterans is in the opening plan, and about half a dozen arcade cabinets are going in the front so dinner doubles as a night out.
